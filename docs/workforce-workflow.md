# Daily workforce and posted copies

The Workforce section has separate Rio, ASC, Driscoll and UTRGV calendars, plus a Rio-pool CRNA relief view. All views read the same account-owned day assignments. Call calendars remain independent.

Choose a site and a day. MD slots use that site's roster; temporary clinicians are typed for the day only. Rio CRNAs may be assigned to ASC or temporarily transferred to another facility without changing their home roster. Regular Driscoll CRNAs are maintained separately in Team and excluded from Rio relief. Relief order is explicitly stored; drag or arrow controls move a person in the list. Timing notes are kept exactly as entered.

Every partial edit autosaves. Errors retain edits and stop retries until the user chooses Retry. Navigation and sign-out wait for the save. Untouched days remain blank. Done is a manual progress marker for the whole day, not a coverage certification. Saved history supports preview and explicit restoration of the entire day.

MD post-call is a separate annotation rendered with `*`. It neither counts as another assignment nor marks someone Off. Adding the previous first-call MD requires an explicit click. Off accepts multiple MDs and CRNAs and is private to the account's office workflow.

## Print and save

Select one calendar and its audience. Hospital workforce copies contain only that site's staff. Provider and hospital copies exclude Off, private office notes, roster keys and home-pool metadata from the PDF preview and Excel file. Staffing notes are excluded unless explicitly selected and visible in the preview. CRNA relief is an internal/provider coordination copy; no hospital-wide relief option is provided. Office copies include Off and are labelled private.

Save issued copy stores an immutable, owner-scoped snapshot of that exact workforce preview, with revision and issue date. Later draft edits do not alter it. Select a saved revision to reprint or download it. This does not send or publish schedules to recipients. Call exports also provide audience selection; their snapshots are downloaded, not stored in the workforce issued-copy archive.
